FAQs Section

How does the points system work?
You earn points for completing tasks, referring friends, and participating in challenges.
How do I claim my rewards?
Go to the "Rewards" section, select your reward, and click "Claim". It will be processed within 24 hours.
How is the ranking calculated?
Rank is based on total points, activity streaks, and bonus achievements. It updates daily.
How often is the leaderboard updated?
Leaderboard data is refreshed every midnight IST based on your latest activities.
